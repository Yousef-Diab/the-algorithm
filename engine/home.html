An interactive course built from ICT's Mentorships — 78 lessons across two sections. Every word comes from ICT's mentorship notes and the original ICT video transcripts; every chart is pulled from the notes.

Section 1 — the ICT Core Content — is 38 lessons across 4 months: market maker templates, equilibrium & fair valuation, liquidity, institutional order flow, PD arrays, and the market maker traps. Section 2 — the 2022 Mentorship — is 40 lessons across 6 parts, one per episode: a single stripped-down intraday model taught end to end, from fair value gaps and market structure shifts to the killzones, the daily bias and risk.

How to use this course: work through the lessons in order — the material builds from one lesson to the next. Flip the concept cards, study every chart (click to zoom), then take the lesson check. Your progress and quiz answers are saved in your browser automatically.
